Our Story

Tradition and Care in Every Scone

Having lived in London, we had the chance to visit scone bakeries all over the city.
We opened this shop so that people in Japan can also enjoy traditional British scones — with their signature side crack — together with a proper cup of tea.

Our bakery is located in Sano, a small town in Tochigi Prefecture. Sano is also home to the beloved mascot Sanomaru, the Sano Premium Outlets, the Sano Yakuyoke Daishi temple, and the famous Sano ramen.
If you are visiting the area, we would love for you to stop by.

How to Enjoy

How to Enjoy a Proper Scone

A quick guide to British afternoon-tea style

1

Split it by hand

Skip the knife — gently break the scone in half with your hands, the British way. Splitting it sideways reveals a soft, fluffy crumb.

2

Pile on the cream

Add plenty of clotted cream. Devon style puts the cream on first; Cornish style starts with the jam.

3

Enjoy with tea

Earl Grey or Darjeeling pairs beautifully. A warm cup of tea alongside makes it a truly blissful moment.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to the questions we are asked most often

Where is the shop located?

We are at 74-1 Iga-cho, Sano, Tochigi. It is a 3-minute walk from Sano Station on the JR Ryomo Line and Tobu Sano Line.

Do you have regular closing days?

We have no fixed closing days — our opening days are irregular.

Do you offer mail order or shipping?

We do not currently offer mail order, but we are considering opening an online shop in the future.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept credit cards and electronic payments. We do not accept cash.
